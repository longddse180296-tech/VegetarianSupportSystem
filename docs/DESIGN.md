## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a clinical yet deeply empathetic presence tailored for modern Vietnamese users seeking sustainable plant-based nutrition, personalized BMI health management, and proactive AI dietary guidance. 

### Brand Archetype & Emotional Tone
- **Precision with Warmth:** Blends scientific nutritional credibility (data-driven BMI, macronutrient tracking, AI consultation) with biological vitality. It avoids sterile hospital clinicalism while firmly separating itself from food delivery or rustic temple-style vegetarian restaurants.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Promotes a calm, mindful mental state through generous whitespace, breathability, structured layouts, and deliberate content pacing.
- **Friendly Technologist:** AI features are integrated as conversational, non-intrusive health companions rather than cold algorithms or overwhelming robotic chatbots.

### Visual Design Movement
- **Modern Clean Organic Minimalism:** Strict typography scales, crisp low-contrast borders, purposeful emerald accents, and serene off-white tinted micro-surfaces. Elevation relies on subtle, daylight-diffused ambient shadowing and soft tonal shifts rather than heavy ornamental gradients.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to invoke physiological vitality, fresh botanical sustenance, and surgical legibility across digital screens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Green (`#2E7D32`):** The primary action and brand color. Used for key call-to-actions, active navigation highlights, vital health indicators, and primary chart anchors.
- **Dark Green (`#1B5E20`):** Grounded botanical deep tone. Applied to active states, pressed button states, prominent hero titles, and high-emphasis analytical markers.
- **Soft Green (`#E8F5E9`):** Gentle botanical wash. Used for card backgrounds, success banners, selected states, badge fills, and conversational AI message bubbles.
- **Main Text (`#1F2937`):** Slate-charcoal for optimal optical contrast without the eye strain of pure `#000000`. Ensures high legibility for long-form nutritional guides and health metrics.
- **Secondary Text (`#6B7280`):** Mid-tone neutral for secondary labels, helper descriptions, timestamps, and dietary metadata.
- **Base Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white canvas providing baseline contrast.
- **Section Background (`#F8FAF8`):** Subtle organic cool-grey tint used to differentiate content regions, alternating blocks, and dashboard side panels.
- **Structural Border (`#E5E7EB`):** Subtle division for cards, table dividers, input fields, and modular separators.
- **Error Tone (`#DC2626`):** Standard validation alerts, excessive calorie alerts, or contraindicated food warnings.
- **Warning Tone (`#D97706`):** Micronutrient deficiencies, hydration reminders, or transitional dietary suggestions.

### Functional Roles & Contrast Ratios
All primary action combinations against white/soft-green backgrounds maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 (WCAG AA standard), with critical body text meeting WCAG AAA standard (7:1+).

## Typography

Inter serves as the foundational typeface across all viewports. Its tall x-height, neutral geometric skeleton, and Vietnamese diacritic glyph support make it optimal for nutritional data visualization and conversational AI dialogues.

### Type Hierarchy & Tone Guidance
- **Headings (Display, H1-H3):** Rendered in Inter Bold (`700`) or SemiBold (`600`) with tight letter-spacing to command respect without aggression. Never use italicized or condensed treatments for health headings.
- **Nutritional Readouts & Metric Values:** Numerical representations of BMI, calorie limits, and macronutrient distributions adopt Inter SemiBold (`600`) with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve alignment across dashboards.
- **Body & Editorial:** Rendered in Inter Regular (`400`) at comfortable line heights (1.5–1.55x) to maintain effortless readability across Vietnamese multi-accent marks (`dấu hỏi`, `ngã`, `nặng`).
- **Interactive Triggers & Actions:** Medium (`500`) to SemiBold (`600`) ensure tactile legibility on buttons, navigation bars, and filter pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural grid system, designed to accommodate complex health monitoring panels, personalized dietary roadmaps, and AI meal planners.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Max Container:** Fixed constraint of `1200px` centered on standard `1440px` viewports, flanked by flexible outer gutters to prevent optical dispersion of vital data.
- **Fixed Header:** Strict `72px` sticky top elevation with a clean white base (`#FFFFFF`) and a subtle bottom border (`#E5E7EB`). Ensures access to AI consultation shortcuts, user profile, and BMI indicators.
- **12-Column Responsive Grid:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `24px` gutters, `1200px` centered max-width.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `20px` gutters, fluid width with `24px` outer screen padding.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `16px` gutters, fluid width with `16px` outer screen margins.
- **Vertical Flow Rhythm:** Distinct visual modules are grouped using `Section Background` (`#F8FAF8`) with padding steps of `64px` to `80px` on desktop, scaling down to `40px` on mobile. Card interiors enforce an internal padding rhythm of `20px` to `24px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on balanced ambient lighting and pristine surface boundaries rather than heavy skeuomorphism or artificial depth.

### Spatial Depths
- **Flat Surface (Level 0):** Used for base page canvas (`#FFFFFF`) and alternating content bands (`#F8FAF8`). Contains no shadow.
- **Subtle Ambient Elevation (Level 1 - Cards & Metric Tiles):**
  - Shadow token: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.02)`
  - Paired with a precise 1px border (`#E5E7EB`) to retain crisp boundaries under varying monitor calibrations.
- **Hover & Focused Interactive Elevation (Level 2 - Actionable Cards, Modals, Menus):**
  - Shadow token: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04)`
  - Features a deliberate, warm botanical-tinted micro-cast to elevate the primary action.
- **Floating Overlays (Level 3 - Fixed Navigation & AI Floating Drawer):**
  - Sticky header (`72px`): Clean `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` with a solid `#E5E7EB` bottom border.
  - Active AI Assistant Floating Panel: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates intentional, organic corner geometry to express friendliness, physical comfort, and modern software craft.

### Corner Radii Architecture
- **Buttons & Interactive Action Triggers:** Standardized at `10px`. Delivers a firm yet softly sculpted interactive hit zone.
- **Text Inputs, Form Selectors & Dropdowns:** Standardized at `10px` for total geometric harmony alongside actionable buttons.
- **Interactive Dashboard Cards, Nutritive Metric Tiles & AI Bubbles:** Standardized at `16px`. Softens data density and frames caloric/nutritional statistics comfortably.
- **Large Hero Sections, Feature Banners & Modals:** Standardized at `20px`. Frames high-level macro-content and full-width graphical onboarding sequences.
- **Nutrient Tag & Chip System:** Standardized at `9999px` (Full Pill) to differentiate lightweight dynamic filters from actionable system buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2E7D32`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `10px`, font Inter SemiBold (`label-md` or `label-lg`). Hover: `#1B5E20`. Active: `#1B5E20` with scale `0.98`. Focus: 3px outline in `#E8F5E9` with `#2E7D32` focus-ring.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Background `#E8F5E9`, text `#2E7D32`, border-radius `10px`, no border. Hover: `#DCF2DE`. Focus: 3px outline in `#2E7D32`.
- **Outline / Minimal:** Background transparent, text `#1F2937`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, border-radius `10px`. Hover: background `#F8FAF8`, border `#2E7D32`, text `#2E7D32`.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, border-radius `10px`, typography `body-md`, padding `0 16px`. Active/Focus: border `1.5px solid #2E7D32`, soft glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px #E8F5E9`.
- **Error States:** Border `#DC2626`, helper message in `#DC2626` using `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** `20px x 20px` square with `4px` radius. Unchecked: border `1.5px solid #E5E7EB`. Checked: background `#2E7D32`, white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** `20px x 20px` circle. Selected: white inner circle surrounded by `#2E7D32` ring.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Dietary Filter Chips:** Height `32px`, border-radius `9999px`, typography `label-sm`. Default: background `#F8FAF8`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#6B7280`. Selected state: background `#E8F5E9`, border `1px solid #2E7D32`, text `#2E7D32`.

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Metric & Recipe Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, border-radius `16px`, ambient level-1 shadow. Padding `20px` or `24px`.
- **Hero / Assessment Banner:** Background `#F8FAF8` or linear gradient (`#F8FAF8` to `#E8F5E9`), border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, border-radius `20px`, padding `32px` to `48px`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **BMI Health Spectrum Bar:** Linear track (`8px` height, radius `9999px`) divided into standard Asian-Pacific BMI thresholds (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese). Active indicator rendered as a floating `16px` circular pin with a tool-tipped readout.
- **AI Nutrition Companion Module:** Embedded assistant card with an animated organic pulsating beacon in `#2E7D32`. Conversational bubbles from the AI are surfaced in `#E8F5E9` with `#1F2937` text; user prompts in `#FFFFFF` with `#E5E7EB` border. Quick prompt chips styled as pill buttons lined along the bottom.
